Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What programs does Esteem Academy offer?+

Cosmetology/Barbering, Master Esthetician, Nail Technology, Master Barbering/Hair Design and Eyelash & Eyebrow Technology.

When does the next class start?+

Our next cosmetology start is December 1, 2026, and new classes start throughout 2027. Send your info and we’ll help you find the start that fits your program.

How long does beauty school take?+

It depends on the program and schedule you choose. Cosmetology/Barbering is 1,250 clock hours and can be completed in as little as 9 months full-time or 15 months part-time. Master Esthetician (1,200 hours) can be completed in as little as 9 months full-time, Master Barbering/Hair Design (1,000 hours) in as little as 8 months full-time, Nail Technology (300 hours) in as little as 15 weeks and Eyelash & Eyebrow Technology (270 hours) in as little as 14 weeks.

Do I need any beauty experience?+

No prior beauty experience is needed. To enroll, you’ll need a high school diploma, GED or the equivalent.

How big are your classes?+

Classes are limited to 8 students, so you get plenty of one-on-one time with licensed instructors.

How do I pay for beauty school?+

Our admissions team will walk you through tuition and your payment options, including financing through our partner, Fortify Education. Esteem Academy does not offer federal financial aid.

Can I tour the school before I enroll?+

Yes. Visit our South Ogden campus to see the classrooms and salon floor and get your questions answered in person.
