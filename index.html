Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What programs does Esteem Academy offer?+

We offer Cosmetology/Barbering, Master Esthetician, Nail Technology, Master Barbering/Hair Design and Eyelash & Eyebrow Technology programs.

When does the next class start?+

Our next cosmetology programs start December 1, 2026, with 10 more start dates throughout 2027. Request info to find the date that works for you.

How big are your classes?+

We keep classes small so every student gets one-on-one instruction and close support from our educators.

Do you offer financing options?+

Yes. Our admissions team can walk you through the financing options available and help you plan your next steps.

What makes Esteem different?+

Along with hands-on beauty training, you’ll learn business development skills and join weekly 30-minute sessions focused on life skills, all in a positive, professional private-school setting.

Can I tour the school before I enroll?+

Absolutely! We’d love to show you around. Call (801) 689-3031 or fill out the form to schedule your tour.
